# 💰 Community Server Fund — Financial Transparency

> Every dollar is tracked. Every purchase is documented. You'll always know exactly where the money is.

---

## How Donations Work

### Platform
Donations are collected via **Ko-fi** (ko-fi.com).

- No platform fee on one-time donations (Ko-fi takes 0%)
- Payment processing fee: ~2.9% + $0.30 per transaction (PayPal/Stripe)
- You can donate any amount — there are no minimum or maximum limits

### Campaign Window
The campaign runs for **60 days** from the launch date. When the window closes, we purchase the build matching the highest funded tier.

If we don't reach Tier 1 ($300) by the end of the campaign, **all donations are refunded in full**.

---

## Where the Money Goes

100% of donations (after payment processing fees) go toward server hardware. There are no salaries, no "admin fees," no middlemen.

| Category | What It Covers |
|----------|---------------|
| **Server hardware** | CPU, motherboard, RAM, cooler, PSU, storage, networking |
| **Shipping & tax** | Estimated ~8-10% on top of component prices |
| **Payment processing fees** | ~2.9% + $0.30 per transaction (unavoidable) |

The server operator covers all ongoing costs (electricity, internet, domain) out of pocket. Your donation is a one-time contribution to hardware only.

---

## Tracking & Reporting

### During the Campaign
- **Running total** updated in Discord (#funding-progress channel) after every donation
- **Tier progress bar** showing how close we are to the next milestone
- **Weekly summary** posted every Sunday with total raised, number of donors, and next milestone target

### After Purchase
- **Full receipt post** — itemized list of every component purchased, where it was bought, and exactly what was paid (including tax and shipping)
- **Remaining balance report** — if any funds are left over after the purchase, we'll disclose the amount and hold it transparently for future upgrades or maintenance

## FAQ

**What if we land between milestones?**
We purchase at the last fully-funded milestone. Any remaining funds are held for the next upgrade and disclosed publicly.

**What if prices change before we buy?**
Prices are estimates based on June 2026 market conditions. If components are cheaper than expected, surplus goes toward the next milestone. If more expensive, we'll adjust the build to stay within budget and explain what changed.

**What if the campaign doesn't hit Tier 1?**
All donations are refunded in full. No one loses money.

**Can I get a refund after Tier 1 is reached?**
Once the campaign reaches Tier 1 ($300), funds are committed to the build. Individual refunds are not available after this point. This is stated upfront before you donate.

**Do you profit from this?**
No. The operator receives no compensation. All funds go to hardware. Ongoing costs (electricity, internet, domain) are covered by the operator personally.
